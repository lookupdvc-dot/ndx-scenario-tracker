What the valuations say

Read: supportive, with a catch. A 20% price fall with rising earnings has taken the forward multiple from 23× to about 19×, below its 10-year average. This is nothing like 2000, when the index traded on more than 100× trailing earnings.

The catch is the "E". The multiple is only cheap if AI earnings hold. Semiconductor profits are cyclical, and a low P/E on peak earnings is the classic trap. If hyperscalers cut 2027 capex, forward earnings fall and the same price looks expensive again.

Bonds compete. The index's earnings yield (about 5.2%) is barely above the 10-year Treasury (4.95%). Equities are no longer cheap against bonds, even after the fall.

      Fundamental read

      Earnings are strong and valuations have reset, which argues against a 2000-style collapse. But the three things that set the multiple are all unfavourable or uncertain: the Fed is hiking, state-level policy threatens the data-centre build-out, and 2027 capex guidance does not arrive until late January. Fundamentals support the base case: further downside as multiples and sentiment adjust, with a floor from earnings unless capex is cut.
